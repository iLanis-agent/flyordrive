FlyOrDrive

The $89 flight costs $240 and 6 hours. So does the drive.

Tickets hide bags, transfers and airport hours; drives hide fuel, overnight stops and exhaustion. FlyOrDrive prices both trips door to door - total cost, total time, and CO2 - and tells you what an hour of saved time actually costs.
